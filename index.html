How to Play

Every mode revolves around the same core action: land a kill with a specific weapon in Rivals, then mark it off here. What that unlocks differs by mode.

Grid Bingo / All

Tap a weapon's tile after you land a kill with it. Race: first to clear their whole card wins. Points: scored below. Team rounds can add an underdog multiplier that boosts a smaller team's points, and an uneven team's smaller side starts with a few free handicap tiles already marked (those never score anything themselves — just a head start).

Per tile10 pts
Per completed line100 pts
Full clear+250 pts

Tic-Tac-Toe

Every square on the shared board is assigned a weapon — land a kill with it to claim that square for your side. First side to complete a row, column, or diagonal wins. Take-backs, if enabled, lets either side reclaim a square the opponent already holds instead of only ever claiming empty ones.

Claim (take-backs on)20 pts
Claim (take-backs off)30 pts
Stealing an opponent's square (Allow Ties off)+10 pts
Stealing an opponent's square (Allow Ties on)+15 pts
Win+250 pts

Battleship

Place your fleet on your own grid during placement — nobody but your own side can see it. Once both sides ready up, every weapon on the board gets assigned to one square each; land a kill with a weapon to attack the matching square on the opponent's grid. Sink their entire fleet to win.

Per attack10 pts
Hit bonus+5 pts
Sinking a ship+100 pts
Win+250 pts

Shared round-start options

  • Exclude weapons — remove specific weapons from the pool before a round starts.
  • Max hard weapons — caps how many weapons tagged "Hard" (via Weapon Difficulty) can end up on a card or in the pool.
  • Free square — everyone starts with one random square already marked/claimed; its difficulty bias skews that freebie toward easier, harder, or purely random weapons.
  • Spectators — controls what someone watching without a board of their own can see: Progress Only (masked until marked/hit) or Fully Visible.
